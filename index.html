Wood Classics LLC

Custom cabinetry, built-ins, bunks, and furniture

Wood Classics is a custom woodshop in Springville, Utah, serving Utah County, Salt Lake County, and the Park City area. We specialize in high-quality cabinetry, built-in bunk rooms, custom furniture, and specialty woodwork. Every project is thoughtfully designed and built with an emphasis on craftsmanship, functionality, and lasting quality.
